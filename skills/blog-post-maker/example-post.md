WhatsApp is where your customers already are — but most local businesses still send generic, forgettable messages. In this guide we break down 7 template patterns that consistently earn replies, along with when to send them and how to automate the follow-ups.

## Why WhatsApp beats email for local business

Open rates on WhatsApp routinely cross 90% versus 20–30% for email. For a clinic, salon or real-estate agent, that difference is the gap between a booked appointment and a missed one.

> The best template is the one your customer can reply to in a single tap.

## The 7 template patterns

1. **New-lead welcome** — acknowledge fast, attach value (brochure/menu/pricelist).
2. **Appointment reminder** — reduce no-shows with a clear date/time + reschedule option.
3. **Festival / seasonal offer** — time-boxed discount with one CTA.
4. **Payment / renewal reminder** — polite, specific, with a pay link.
5. **Review request** — sent right after a positive experience.
6. **Win-back** — re-engage customers who went quiet.
7. **Upsell / cross-sell** — recommend the natural next purchase.

### Example: new-lead welcome

```text
Namaste {{name}} ji! 🙏 Thanks for your interest in {{project}}.
Brochure & pricing attached. Free for a quick call today?
```

## Timing that works in India

| Message type | Best window (IST) |
| --- | --- |
| Welcome / lead | Within 5 minutes of the enquiry |
| Reminders | 10:00–12:00 or 17:00–19:00 |
| Offers | Thu–Sat evening |

## Automate the boring parts

Use variables like `{{name}}` and `{{date}}` so one template serves hundreds of customers, and chain a reminder 24 hours after the welcome message.
