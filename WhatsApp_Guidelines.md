## 2. Meta WhatsApp Official Template Categories

Meta categorizes all WhatsApp Business API message templates into 3 strict official categories:

### A. Utility Templates (Transactional)
*Messages triggered by a customer's explicit action, order, or request:*
- **Order Confirmation:** Instant receipt after order placement.
- **Payment Status:** Receipt confirmation or payment failure alert.
- **Delivery Updates:** Dispatch, out-for-delivery, and tracking links.
- **Pickup Reminders:** Counter pickup ready notifications.
- **Invoice Sharing:** Tax invoice PDF link.
- **Return & Replacement Updates:** Status of exchange/refund request.
- **Warranty & Repair Updates:** Service center job sheet status.
- **Appointment Confirmation:** Booking confirmation with date & time.

### B. Marketing Templates (Promotions & Re-engagement)
*Messages designed to drive sales, brand awareness, and customer retention:*
- **Offers & Discounts:** Promo codes, flash sales, seasonal deals.
- **New Arrivals:** Launch announcements and collection previews.
- **Product Recommendations:** Cross-sell, upsell, and personalized recommendations.
- **Festival Sales:** Diwali, Eid, Christmas, and holiday campaigns.
- **Back-in-Stock Promotions:** Restocked notification for wishlisted items.
- **Cart Recovery:** Abandoned cart recovery nudges.
- **Loyalty Promotions:** Reward points, tier upgrades, birthday gifts.
- **Re-engagement Messages:** Inactive customer comeback vouchers (15/30/60/90 days).
- **Event Invitations:** Store launch, fashion show, or bridal trial invites.

### C. Authentication Templates (Security & OTP)
*Messages used for user authentication and account protection:*
- **OTP Verification:** One-time passwords for mobile verification.
- **Login Verification:** Two-factor authentication codes.
- **Account Recovery Code:** Password reset verification link.
- **Payment Verification:** Transaction confirmation OTP.
- **Pickup Verification OTP:** Store pickup verification code.

## 3. Mandatory WhatsApp Business Messaging Guidelines & Compliance Rules

To prevent WhatsApp account ban, maintain high sender quality rating, and respect user privacy, all templates must adhere to the following rules:

1. **Clear Customer Opt-In:** Always obtain explicit customer opt-in before sending marketing or promotional broadcasts.
2. **Mandatory Unsubscribe Option:** Include a clear `"Stop Promotions"` / `"Unsubscribe"` button in all marketing templates.
3. **No Promo Content in Utility Messages:** Utility (transactional) templates must strictly contain order/delivery info without cross-promotional ads or unrelated discounts.
4. **Transparent Pricing & Validity:** State product prices, exact discount percentages, and coupon expiry dates clearly.
5. **No Fake Urgency or Misleading Claims:** Avoid manipulative tactics such as fake countdown timers or unverified medical/financial promises.
6. **Verified & Secure Links Only:** Always use HTTPS encrypted, official domain links (`https://replyflow.procsctools.in` or verified business URLs).
7. **NEVER Ask for Sensitive Data:** Never request OTPs, PINs, CVVs, netbanking passwords, or credit card numbers via chat.
8. **Verify Transaction Details:** Always verify Order ID, invoice amount, customer name, and delivery address before sending order updates.
9. **Zero Spam Tolerance:** Do not send repetitive promotional messages to the same customer within a short timeframe.
10. **Regulatory Compliance:** Adhere strictly to government and industry regulations for medical, pharmaceutical, financial, and age-restricted products.
